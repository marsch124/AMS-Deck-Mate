# AMS Deck Mate

A small Mac menu bar app, version 0.25 (build 35), with Deck Mate for the iPhone and iPad at 0.34 (build 40). It drives a Stream Deck box with 15 keys straight over its cable, without Elgato's app and without a subscription.

Each key can open an app, a link or a file, run a Raycast command, press a keyboard shortcut, type text, control music and sound, move to another page or page group, or do several things in a row. The keys change by themselves when another app comes to the front. A floating window on the screen shows the same 15 keys, so the keys also work with no box plugged in. The keys on screen can also work as a virtual deck: press Control-Option-D, the keys appear under the mouse pointer, click one and they are gone. Since 0.3 there is also Deck Mate for the iPhone (TestFlight): it shows the same 15 keys, follows the page group and page the Mac shows, and a tap on a key makes the Mac do it. It finds the Mac by itself on the same Wi-Fi (or with the Mac on the iPhone's hotspot). Since 0.4 the iPhone keys sit low near the thumb, swipe between pages, light up when pressed and say what ran. Since 0.5 (iPhone and iPad app, now 0.8, with settings for look, clock, touch and what shows) Deck Mate is also a proper iPad app, made for the iPad mini: it fills the whole screen in all four ways round, with larger keys that grow as far as the screen or window allows, low and centred. The keys on screen can be used from the keyboard (since 0.4.1 each key shows its letter while the keyboard is theirs), Set up keys can drag, copy, paste, duplicate, undo and redo (since 0.5 an "All pages" button shows every page of every page group as a small picture), and broken keys show red. At first start the keys from Elgato's app are brought over. Since 0.5 a key can have a second action on a long press (marked by a small white dot), a row of 5 favourites stays on every page on the iPhone and iPad, an iPhone or iPad can use its own keys instead of following the Mac, and the iPhone and iPad can show the Mac's battery, the app in front and a music strip (needs iPhone and iPad app 0.7). Since 0.6 (Mac) and 0.8 (iPad), an iPad held on its side also shows a navigation picture on the left: every page group with every page as a small picture, and a tap goes there. Since 0.20 (iPhone and iPad) a swipe to the left on that picture hides it, and the keys grow into the room; a thin purple "Pages" tab at the left edge, tapped or pulled to the right, brings it back. Since 0.21, with the picture hidden, the favourites (now round keys) stand in a column beside that tab and the keys grow.

Since 0.8 there are two more keys, "Last copied text" (pastes one of your last copied texts, kept only in memory) and "Recent file" (opens a file you opened lately); Settings can bring back an earlier safety copy of your setup, and a damaged keys file repairs itself from the newest readable copy. The link to the iPhone and iPad is now private (encrypted) and asks you to check a 4-digit number the first time, so the iPhone and iPad app 0.11 is needed with Mac 0.8.

Since 0.9 the Mac keeps a weekly copy of your keys in iCloud Drive (the newest 8), "Last copied text" has a fourth choice, "A list to pick from", and the iPhone and iPad app 0.12 shows a small lock beside the connection dot while the line is private. The code is public on GitHub and the rule tests run there by themselves at every change.

Since 0.10 there are six more kinds of key (Timer, Switch for dark mode or mute, Run an Apple Shortcut, List to pick from, Open app, Microphone light), a "Wait" step for chains, a Find a key search on the keys on screen (Space or /), a "Key use" list in Settings, and four ready-made page groups (Finder, Safari, Notion, Fusion).

Since 0.11 there are six more kinds of key: Next event, Weather, Due today (originally OmniFocus), Album cover (Spotify), Body Battery and Desk (puts app windows back in place). Five of them show a value in large words on the key.

Since 0.12 there are five more kinds of key that show a value: Garmin number (steps, sleep, resting pulse, stress, workouts, from your own Garmin board on the Mac), Mac at a glance (battery, free space, busy light), World clock, Sunrise and sunset, and Free until (from your calendar). A glance key can now change its colour with its value, and the long press for a town works on World clock and Sunrise and sunset too.

Since 0.13 there are six more kinds of key: On the Mac (lock the Mac, show the desktop, quit all apps, empty the bin, eject all disks, paste as plain text), Join the meeting, Spotify playlist, Search for the copied text, Counter and Stopwatch (a long press sets them back to 0). The Switch key can also keep the Mac awake.

Since 0.14 there are two more kinds of key: PARAGON (shows the Inbox count or the next task, adds a task from the copied text, or takes a quick note) and Focus rounds (starts a PomoTimer template and counts the steps on the key). Due today now counts PARAGON tasks instead of OmniFocus, the Spotify playlist key shows the cover, and Quit all apps starts with the apps open now. The PARAGON keys only read a small file PARAGON writes for its widget and use its own links.

Since 0.15 the safety copies of your keys last longer (the newest 20, one for each of the last 30 days, and some before big changes), a damaged keys file can also be repaired from the weekly iCloud Drive copy, a copied password is no longer used by the search, AI and PARAGON keys, and strangers on the same Wi-Fi are limited. Red is kept for real trouble (Focus rounds, Body Battery, stress and resting pulse are calmer), the Garmin keys say "Board off" and "As of 06:29", and the Garmin number key has two gym numbers. "What it does" is a list with a search field, you can drop an app, a file, a folder or a web address on an empty key, and every key can be pressed from Raycast or by a link that carries this Mac's own pass. The iPhone and iPad (app 0.17) notice a dead line within 12 seconds, and only changed keys travel.

Since 0.16 there are three more kinds of key (45 in all): Breathing pacer (a column that fills and empties while you breathe), Progress bar (part of a row) (several keys side by side make one bar for the focus round or the timer), and Often used key (shows and presses one of the keys you press most). A PARAGON key "Now on my plan" shows the block of your day, and a ready-made page group "My AMS apps" has 12 link keys. Timers show whole minutes ("21 min") and end with one softer sound. Set up keys says at once when a link, a town or a Spotify link is wrong, "New key from…" in the menu makes a key from the app in front, a copied link or the last file, the deck suggests a key for an app you use often, and "Save as PDF…" prints every page group. A page group can show at set hours, the box can dim in the evening, and Apple's Shortcuts app can press keys. Safer: the allowed iPhones' secrets moved to the Mac's Keychain, a failed save says so, the weekly iCloud copy also holds the key pictures, scripts have a time limit, and Return now cancels in every question. Keys nobody sees rest, and checks pause while the screen sleeps. The iPhone and iPad (app 0.18) always show a note for a key that failed, search for the Mac more gently, dim the resting clock and can rest sooner in the evening.

Since 0.18 the Switch key has three fan choices (Fans: Automatic, Cool and Full) that ask AMS Fan Menu (0.2 or newer) for the fan speed and show which is on; the iPhone and iPad app needs no update.

Since 0.19 the Cool and Full fan keys last 10 minutes (AMS Fan Menu 0.3.1 or newer), "Set up keys" is taller so nothing is cut off, and each page group has four places for the pages an iPad shows at once (an iPhone: two). The iPhone and iPad app 0.31 has a switch between one page and several on top of the navigation picture, the pull from the left edge also on an iPhone with two pages, and bigger keys there.

Since 0.25 (Mac; the iPhone and iPad app stays 0.33) there is one more kind of key, "Steward: to decide": it shows how many bank rows wait in AMS Financial Steward's To decide (the number in large figures with "to decide" in amber; "0" with "all decided" in green; "–" with "Open Steward" in grey-blue when Steward has not counted for 36 hours). A press opens Steward at To decide. It needs AMS Financial Steward 0.21 or later, which leaves a tiny note with only the count and the time (no amounts, no names); Deck Mate reads it once a minute, only while such a key is on a page, and never writes it. The first time, macOS may ask whether AMS Deck Mate may access data from other apps: Allow. Its colour says something, so it has no colour of its own.

Since 0.24 (Mac; the iPhone and iPad app stays 0.33) a key can wear a colour of its own: in Set up keys, beside the Icon, there is a "Colour" well and a small button "Kind's colour" that puts the colour of its kind back. Keys whose colour already says something (the microphone light, Weather, Due today, PARAGON, Training today, Focus, Body Battery, the Garmin numbers, Mac now, World clock, Free until and the progress bars) keep it. The colour reaches the phone as part of the key picture drawn by the Mac. So a page group made for one app can wear that app's colours.

Since 0.23 (Mac) and 0.33 (iPhone and iPad) there is one more kind of key, "Training today": it shows today's session from AMS Triathlon Training 2.8 or later ("45 min" with "Run · to do", "Done" in green, "Missed", "Rest", or "–" with "Open Triathlon" when nothing is fresh). A press on the iPhone logs the session as done; on the Mac, the Stream Deck and the iPad it opens the session in AMS Triathlon Training. The app adds no key to your pages by itself.

Since 0.22 the Switch key has a ninth choice, "Fans: fan speed": a key that shows how fast the Mac's fans turn live ("Fans 1,650" turns per minute, the faster fan; "Fans off" when they stand still, "Fans –" when there is no fresh reading) from the same AMS Fan Menu 0.6 note, with no switch mark; a press opens Fan Menu's graph. The app adds no key to your pages by itself. The iPhone and iPad app needs no update.

Since 0.21 the Switch key has an eighth choice, "Fans: chip temperature": a key that shows the Mac's chip temperature live ("Chip 56°", "Chip –" when there is no fresh reading) from AMS Fan Menu 0.6 or newer, with no switch mark; a press opens Fan Menu's graph. The iPhone and iPad app needs no update.

Since 0.20 (iPhone and iPad 0.32) the fan key that is on shows the minutes left ("Cool · 8 min"), every key can have a double press (two presses within a third of a second, marked by a small white "×2"; a key that has one waits a third of a second, other keys act at once), and keys say in words what they do: a small plate when the pointer rests on a key on the Mac, and an "Explain" button on the iPhone and iPad that reads a key without pressing it.

Since 0.17 (iPhone and iPad 0.19) there is "Several pages at once" for using the iPhone or iPad without the box: 2 pages on an iPhone, 4 on an iPad, arranged for the biggest keys. Since 0.17.1 (iPhone and iPad 0.19.1) the page group's folders fill the empty spots, so a group with one page and three folders still shows 4 pages on an iPad. Both guides also have a part "Signs in this app", and the small buttons now carry words.

## Look
Flat coloured tiles with white hand-drawn icons, one colour per kind of key: apps blue, links teal, Raycast red-orange, shortcuts purple, text amber, music green, moving around grey-blue.

## Note
0.1 was built while the box was away, so driving the real box is not yet tried. The first test is planned for January 2027.

## Build
1. `xcodegen generate`
2. `xcodebuild -scheme "AMS Deck Mate" -configuration Release -derivedDataPath build build`
3. Rule tests: `cd Core && swift test`

The installed app is at `~/Applications/AMS Deck Mate.app`. The written specification is `Docs/SPEC.md`.

## Folders
- `App/` the Mac app
- `Phone/` the iPhone and iPad app (Deck Mate 0.32; 0.5 added the iPad, 0.6 added settings)
- `Core/` the rules (moving around, Raycast links, how the box is spoken to, the link to the iPhone) and their tests
- `Docs/` the specification

The repository is public. It holds no personal data. Apart from the weekly iCloud Drive copy of your keys, nothing leaves the Mac, except that the Weather, World clock and Sunrise and sunset keys ask Open-Meteo for a town's place, time zone, weather and sun times, and the Album cover key asks Spotify's open look-up for a cover by the song's name.
